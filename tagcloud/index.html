Tag Cloud Generator, an introduction

The generator splits a text into words, counts how often each word occurs and draws the words in proportion to their frequency. Words are lower-cased; apostrophes and hyphens inside a word are kept (“don't”, “low-quality”). Words shorter than the minimum number of characters, or occurring less often than the minimum frequency, are left out. With remove, common function words (“the”, “and”, “og”, “och”…) are dropped using a list for the chosen language.

The tag cloud lists the words like a text, in the chosen order. The word cloud packs the most frequent words around the centre; the same text and options always give the same cloud. Font size grows with the square root of the frequency, so one dominant word doesn't shrink the rest. The frequency table and the CSV, TSV and JSON downloads give the exact counts.

A tool from Nordicamo, part of the research project AlterPublics at Roskilde University, hosted by the Digital Media Lab.
